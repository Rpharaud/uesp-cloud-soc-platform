# Quarterly Design Review (QDR)

> **Build Like an Engineer. Think Like an Architect.**

---

## Purpose

The **Quarterly Design Review (QDR)** provides a structured review of the UESP Cloud SOC Platform's architectural evolution over the reporting period.

Its purpose is to evaluate significant engineering decisions, measure architectural progress, assess technical risks, review technical debt, and establish priorities for the next phase of development.

The QDR serves as the executive engineering review for the residency and should summarize—not replace—the detailed engineering artifacts produced throughout the quarter.
