# Risk Register

> **Build Like an Engineer. Think Like an Architect.**

---

## Purpose

The **Risk Register** serves as the official record of all identified risks throughout the Security Engineering & Cloud Architecture Residency (SECAR).

A risk is an uncertain event or condition that, if it occurs, may negatively impact the residency, flagship platform, engineering deliverables, schedule, cost, security, or learning objectives.

The purpose of this register is to identify risks early, evaluate their potential impact, define mitigation strategies, and monitor them throughout the residency.

This register is a living document and shall be reviewed during Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, and Quarterly Design Reviews.

---

## Risk Management Philosophy

Risk cannot be eliminated.

Instead, SECAR seeks to:

* Identify risks early.
* Assess likelihood and impact.
* Reduce risk through engineering decisions.
* Monitor changing conditions.
* Document mitigation strategies.
* Learn from realized risks.

The goal is proactive risk management rather than reactive problem solving.

## Risk Categories

| Category       | Description                                                       |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Architecture   | Risks affecting system design or long-term maintainability        |
| Security       | Risks affecting confidentiality, integrity, or availability       |
| Infrastructure | Risks involving cloud resources, networking, or platform services |
| Schedule       | Risks that may delay sprint or phase completion                   |
| Technical      | Risks associated with technology selection or implementation      |
| Operational    | Risks affecting engineering processes or platform operations      |
| Financial      | Budget, licensing, or cloud cost risks                            |
| Learning       | Risks affecting residency progression or skill acquisition        |
| External       | Risks outside the engineer's direct control                       |

## Review Checklist

During each review cycle, evaluate every active risk by asking:

* Has the likelihood changed?
* Has the impact changed?
* Is mitigation still effective?
* Has the risk been realized?
* Can the risk be closed?
* Does the contingency plan remain appropriate?
* Does this risk require an ADR or introduce technical debt?
